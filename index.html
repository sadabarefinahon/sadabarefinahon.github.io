Where the attention actually goes

Everything else in my life has been a rotation of interests. Tech is the one thing that stuck and kept pulling harder.

What pulls me in

Not one layer of tech, but the connections between them — how a piece of code becomes a running system, how that system is architected to survive load, and how algorithms quietly decide outcomes underneath it all. Data is the material; figuring out how it's shaped, moved, and protected is the actual craft.

How I think

ENTP, which mostly means I get more energy from arguing an idea from five angles than from executing the first one that works. Useful for design, occasionally a liability for finishing things — which is exactly why the CSE degree exists.
